
## Variant 3
- **Name:** Geometri Riang (playful geometry). Every category is a flat shape in its own colour (Sains circle, Esai quarter-circle, Debat two facing half-moons, Teknologi square, Seni triangle); shapes stack into banners, posters and the Wall. The 8-point star (two rotated squares, from Islamic geometric tilework) marks wins.
- **Palette:** chalk #F2F4F3, navy ink #17233F, cobalt #2B50E8, tomato #F0522F, sunflower #FFC21A, leaf #12A26B, pink #FF6FA8. Flat fields only, no gradients, no tints-as-decoration.
- **Type:** Archivo (variable width) only: expanded 125 / weight 800 for display, normal width for UI and body.
- **Layout:** a horizontal deadline ruler (days 0-60, shapes placed on their closing day) as the home signature; the lomba list as a timetable of rows; detail as a split poster (colour field with shape composition + facts); the Wall as an equal-tile colour mosaic poster; profile banner built from the student's interest shapes.
- **Mood:** children's-museum signage meets a Bauhaus poster: bold, flat, cheerful, tidy grid with shapes that break out of it.
## Variant 1
- **Name:** Pucuk Rebung (songket festival). The app as Deli-Malay songket cloth and a school-festival hall: deep jewel-tone bands with gold "pucuk rebung" triangle edges, competitions hung as notched banners (panji), winners wearing sashes (selempang).
- **Palette (jewel, not primaries):** songket maroon #7A1430 (header bands), gold thread #D9A521, fuchsia #C8246B (primary action), emerald #0A6B4E, indigo #2C2F86, kunyit orange #E06A12; ground blush-white #FFF8F5, ink aubergine #2A0D1C.
- **Type:** Shrikhand (fat hand-painted signage display, headings and big numerals only) + Plus Jakarta Sans (UI/body; made in Jakarta).
- **Layout:** stacked full-width cloth bands with zig-zag gold edges; the lomba list as a hall of tall notched banners hung from gold rods (not rows, not cards); detail as one long banner + facts band; Wall as equal tiles each crossed by a diagonal sash; profile with a woven songket band banner.
- **Mood:** festive, proud, local (kenduri / pentas seni energy), warm and hand-lettered, not flat-Bauhaus.

## Variant 2
- **Name:** Papan Tulis (the class chalkboard, flat and modern). The whole app is a dark green board; white index cards are held by round colour "magnets" (the magnet colour = category); the date corner of the board carries today's date and announcements. No chalk texture, no handwriting fonts, no paper.
- **Palette:** board green #1C4638 (ground), deep board #12322A, chalk white #EEF3EA, card white #FFFFFF with board ink #12322A; chalk colours as category/magnet: yellow #FFD447 (primary action), pink #FF8DB3, sky #78C6FF, apricot #FFA25C, mint #7FE0B0.
- **Type:** Dela Gothic One (chunky display) + Onest (UI and body).
- **Layout:** desktop = slim left rail + board + right "date corner" column; lists are cards hung by magnets in a loose grid; detail is one big card on the board with a magnet strip; Wall = the board itself written full of wins, side by side; phone = bottom "chalk tray" tab bar.
- **Mood:** the classroom after the bell: dark, warm, cheeky, high contrast, colour from chalk and magnets only.
